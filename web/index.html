A / SOURCES OF SPREAD

Follow the uncertainty downstream

P10–P90 cumulative response envelopes, fixed order. Labels show main-effect variance shares; increments depend on stage order. Interaction is reported separately. Percentages are shares of attributed variance; the bracketed range is the lowest to highest share across six alternative ways of separating forced change from natural variability, so none of them is a settled value (hover the headline for GCM-bootstrap intervals). Hydrological parameters are parameter sets within one model structure; model structure, groundwater and management assumptions are not sampled, so a small share here does not mean hydrological modelling adds little uncertainty overall.

B / SHIFTING HORIZONS

The future is a range

Change from each chain’s own 1990–2019 calendar-year mean. Dashed line: ensemble-mean smoothed response. Empirical bands are ensemble percentiles and the primary display. Normal-approximation bands are the mean ± 1.645σ from the variance split and assume a bell-shaped spread, which scenario mixtures and skewed runoff may not follow; dotted P05–P95 limits are drawn when the two disagree. Percentile curves are calculated year by year, so the chain forming P90 can change between years: they are not coherent drought trajectories. Internal variability comes from polynomial residuals.

C / SCREENING THRESHOLD

How often is a runoff-change threshold crossed?

A −20% change in annual runoff is a screening level, not an ecological limit, supply failure point or loss of resilience; those need a defined asset, performance measure and operating context. Shares describe this ensemble, not the probability of a scenario. Annual denominators count chain-years; other rules count chains. Each cell also states the number of chains.

Release provenance, limitations & chain list

Scope and limitations

  • Annual catchment runoff only. No river routing, storage, operating rules or water-management arrangements are represented.
  • No seasonal, low-flow, cease-to-flow, inter-event, inundation or post-drought recovery measures. Each needs a defined asset, performance measure and routing context before it can be added.
  • The hydrological dimension varies parameters within one model structure.
  • Synthetic demonstration data: not for planning decisions.
