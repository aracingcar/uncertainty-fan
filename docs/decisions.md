# Decisions

Accepted 2026-10-30 (see [REMEDIATION_GUIDE.md](REMEDIATION_GUIDE.md)).

- **D1** The graphic explains the decade mean by default; single-year is a secondary view.
- **D2** Internal variability is reported as slow (member-to-member response) plus fast (residual).
- **D3** Slicers do not re-run variance shares; unsliced panels are labelled "full ensemble".
